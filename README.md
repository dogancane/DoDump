# DoDump 3D

3D simulator for rock dumping through a fall pipe. Predicts whether, where and how often the pipe blocks.

## What it can do

- Simulate rock dumping through a moonpool bucket fall pipe or an inclined side pipe, in full 3D
- Use measured rock grades or standard grade envelopes, with realistic stone shapes
- Model the water inside the pipe, the dry fall in the moonpool and the water level drawdown
- Feed rock by drop or by conveyor discharge, evenly, in surges or intermittently
- Include vessel roll, pitch and heave, pipe tilt, current and the FP ROV
- Simulate the lower string and pipe outlet: outlet nozzle and the rock berm or seabed below it
- Show the rock share of the pipe volume, the measure of how dense the flow is
- Detect restrictions, domes and persistent blockages, with their location and the stones involved
- Show the run live in a 3D view with charts
- Run many simulations in the background and give the blockage probability per rock grade and pipe size
- Report wall impacts per bucket and stone speed at the water surface
- Save a probability study as a PDF report
- Compare runs and replay any run
- Check the engine against a published 3D clogging benchmark
